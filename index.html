Who I Am

About Me

I'm a full stack developer from Kathmandu who enjoys turning complex problems into simple, working software. My strongest ground is the front end: Angular, TypeScript, RxJS and NG-ZORRO. I also build the APIs and databases behind them with ASP.NET Core and SQL Server.

I have worked on government digital platforms for waste management and landfill monitoring used in Kathmandu Metropolitan City, covering dashboards, API integration, maps and data visualization. I'm now building inventory and accounting software where the data model and the business rules matter as much as the screens.

I'm currently deepening my backend skills in ASP.NET Core Web API, Clean Architecture, Entity Framework Core and SQL Server, and I'm open to collaborating on Angular, .NET and full stack projects.
